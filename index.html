How to play

Tap the floor to move. Tap a guard to sneak up and silently take them down.

Amber cones are vision. Walls and crates block sight. Dark-blue shadow tiles shrink how far guards can see you.

If the meter fills, guards chase you — break line of sight and hide until they give up. Being caught ends the mission.

Eliminate every guard, then reach the glowing extraction tile.
